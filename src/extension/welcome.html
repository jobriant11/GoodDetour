Welcome

Put a thoughtful turn between impulse and attention.

Good Detour redirects only the sites you choose. Routes stay in this browser unless you opt in to Chrome Sync; the developer never receives them. There is no browsing-history upload or hidden tracking.

1. Choose a habitTry cnn.com, foxnews.com, or any site you visit on autopilot.
2. Pick a better routeChoose AP, NPR, Hacker News, a TED channel, or your own URL.
3. Keep the pauseUse the calm landing page, or skip it for a direct redirect.

Permission, in plain English

Access is requested one site at a time.

When you save a rule, Chrome asks whether Good Detour may act on that domain. It uses that access only to perform the redirect you created. You can revoke access or remove the extension at any time.
