Effective August 19, 2026

Privacy policy

Good Detour is designed so your redirect choices and browsing behavior never go to the developer. Rules stay on one browser unless you choose Chrome Sync.

What the extension stores

The extension stores the redirect rules you create, your pause-page preferences, whether redirects are enabled, and a total count of pause pages shown. By default, Chrome stores this information locally in the extension's private storage on your device.

Optional Chrome Sync

If you enable Chrome Sync inside Good Detour, Chrome Sync stores and synchronizes your rule definitions, enabled setting, and pause-page preferences through your signed-in Google account. The local pause count is never synced. The developer cannot access your Chrome Sync data. Chrome advises that extension sync storage should not hold confidential data, so do not put sensitive or private URLs in synced rules. Each Chrome browser still requires its own approval for the source-site permissions used by synced rules. You can turn this feature off at any time; doing so stops using the synced copy on that browser but does not delete the copy used by your other signed-in Chrome browsers.

What the extension does not collect

This version does not send visited domains, browsing history, page content, identifiers, or the local pause count to the developer. It contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking SDK. When you opt in to Chrome Sync, only the rule domains and destination URLs you entered plus related preferences are handled by Google's Chrome Sync service.

Permissions

Good Detour requests access to a domain only after you add that domain as the source of a redirect. That access is used to apply the redirect you requested. The extension uses Chrome's declarative network request system so the browser can perform the redirect without giving the extension the contents of each network request.

Sharing and sale

Good Detour does not sell or rent user data, and the developer does not receive it. Browsing activity is not used for advertising, profiling, creditworthiness, or any unrelated purpose. Optional synchronization is performed by Google's Chrome Sync service at your request.

Your choices

You may turn off a rule, turn off all redirects, disable Chrome Sync on a browser, export or delete your rules, revoke site access in Chrome, or uninstall the extension. Uninstalling removes that browser's extension storage according to Chrome's behavior; synced data may remain available to other signed-in Chrome browsers until removed through those browsers or your Google account.

Future changes

If a future version introduces other optional remote features, it will explain the data involved in the product interface before collection and request affirmative consent where required. Material privacy changes will not be applied retroactively without notice and any required consent.

Chrome Web Store Limited Use

The use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

For privacy questions, support requests, or deletion questions, contact the Good Detour maintainers through the public support tracker. Do not include private URLs or browsing information in an issue. Security-sensitive reports should follow the repository's security policy.
